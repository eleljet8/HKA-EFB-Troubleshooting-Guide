Managed Apple Account

The Managed Apple Account is created by and linked with your company email account.

A pop-up message may appear on your EFB asking for signing in and verification after you updated your company email password.

Kindly follow the pop-up message to go through the verification process with 2FA authenticator again after password update.

Verification Fail Solution
  1. Open a web browser (Safari / Edge) on your EFB, visit account.apple.com under PRIVATE view.
  2. Sign in with company email and password.

The pop-up message should have been gone.

1. Update Issue Resolution

If you encounter update issue with Web Manuals Reader, please close all the running apps on your EFB, then relaunch the Web Manuals Reader app.

Workaround for Document Checkout Error
  1. Update the Web Manuals app to the latest version via Business app
  2. Fully close (force-quit) the app to clear the stuck queue.
  3. Reopen the app while connected to the internet and allow it to sync.
  4. Download and open the latest revision of the document, then complete the checkout.
3. Re-login Credentials & Setup

If you need to re-login, please use the following:

Domain: hongkongairlines.webmanuals.aero
Username: (your company email)
  • Use your company email password to set up the Authenticator for Single Sign-on.
  • Press “Skip” if prompted for offline PIN setup.
4. Synchronization & Manual Downloads
  • Ensure device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection.
  • Open Web Manuals Reader and tap the Sync button in the top menu to refresh document libraries.
  • Verify that all required operational manuals are fully downloaded for offline reading.

1. Update Troubleshooting Procedure

If you encounter issues during update, please connect to stable Wi-Fi and activate troubleshooting mode:

  1. Go to "Administration" Tab
  2. Enable Trouble Shooting Mode and REMOVE ALL DATA. Full provisioning package will be available for download.
If Missing Information in Settings:

If the above does not solve the issue, some information in settings might be missing. Please Enter Subscription Info, then Download Updates:

i. Tap on MENU > SETTINGS > USER/SUBSCRIPTION INFO

ii. Enter the below parameters:

Username: (your company email)
Server Name: https://ncim-prod.ncim.navblue.cloud
Company: Hong Kong Airlines
Subscription: HKACOM
Device Name: HKAEFB

After registering, tap on Available Updates and download the package.
